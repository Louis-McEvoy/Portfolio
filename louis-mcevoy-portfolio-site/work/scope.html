Scope

Adjustable bone conduction headphones that fit every head and keep calls private.

Year
2026
Type
Individual design project
Focus
Fit, acoustics, CMF
Methods
Research, FDM prototyping, rendering

Problem

Bone conduction headphones come in one or two sizes. They hurt when tight, sound thin when loose and leak sound on calls.

Approach

A pre-tensioned aluminium band with ten locking sizes, and an earpiece built from a Voronoi lattice and a micro-perforated panel.

Result

A premium design for the gym and the office, validated through iterative fit prototypes on real users.

01 · Problem

One size fits nobody

Bone conduction only sounds good when the transducer presses firmly on the cheekbone. Current models come in one or two fixed sizes: too tight and they hurt within the hour, too loose and the sound thins out. People buy third-party straps to compensate.

They also leak sound, which makes them awkward on calls and in offices. Scope is designed to work for the gym, the office and everything in between.

  • BenchmarkedShokz, JBL, Suunto
  • Sizes on market1–2 per model
  • AdjustabilityNone found

02 · Geometry

A band that holds at every size

Circular bands held position but changed clamp force as soon as a slider was added. A bendable gooseneck let users shape the fit, yet transducer vibration and running slowly pushed it out of place.

The final band is rigid aluminium with an angular, pre-tensioned profile, so every extension still loads the cheekbone. A slider locks into ten positions with a utility-knife style detent and cannot creep during use.

  • BandAluminium, pre-tensioned
  • Sizes10 locking positions
  • LockDetent slider

03 · Prototyping

Fit, tested on real heads

A snap-fit rear strap let earpiece variants be swapped in minutes. Early earpieces hit the mastoid process and lifted off the skull, so the next versions were deepened and rounded.

A ball-and-socket test proved the best contact angle differs for every wearer, which confirmed the need for adjustment. A dual-density foam pad behaved as intended but needed stiffer layers. A two-part compliant slider hides unused strap on smaller heads.

04 · Acoustics

Comfort and privacy, engineered into the earpiece

A multi-density Voronoi lattice sits between transducer and skull: soft cells against the head conform to the cheekbone, dense cells at the transducer carry the vibration. The wider pad spreads clamp force, so contact stays firm without pressure points.

Behind the transducer, a micro-perforated aluminium panel absorbs the band that leaks most. Hole size and spacing were set with Maa’s equation to target human speech.

  • Lattice cells4–6 mm
  • Panel thickness1 mm
  • Hole diameter0.2–0.4 mm
  • Target band1.3–3 kHz
  • MethodMaa’s equation

05 · Interface

Controls you can find without looking

User research showed people rarely change volume on the headphones mid-workout, so the volume buttons stay physical but small.

Track selection moves to a textured aluminium dial. Its forward roll follows the natural sweep of the hand past the ear: more tactile than a tap, and harder to trigger by accident.

06 · CMF

Built for sweat, sun and years of use

Brushed aluminium-silicon-magnesium alloy forms the casing and band: strong for its weight, and the brushed finish hides wear. Aluminium keeps its properties through hundreds of thousands of flex cycles, where plastics fatigue.

Transparent and coloured parts are injection-moulded polycarbonate for impact and UV resistance. Medical-grade silicone, overmoulded onto the lattice, is hypoallergenic and sweat-resistant.

  • Band, casingAl-Si-Mg, brushed
  • HousingsPolycarbonate, moulded
  • Skin contactMedical silicone
  • Metal partsDie-cut, bent
