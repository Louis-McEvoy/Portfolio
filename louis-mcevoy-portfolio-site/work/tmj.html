Jaw implant

A lighter jaw implant, proven in simulation for ten years of chewing.

Timeline
Jan – Jul 2025
Analysis
Static, fatigue, modal
Tools
Ansys, Fusion 360
Materials
Ti-6Al-4V, SS316L

Brief

Redesign a temporomandibular joint implant to be lighter while staying durable.

Approach

A honeycomb lattice and weight-reducing cut-outs, analysed in two biocompatible alloys.

Result

All four variants passed the fatigue, frequency and strength requirements.

01 · Requirements

Light, stiff and good for ten years

The implant must be light for comfort, survive a million loading cycles, roughly ten years of chewing, and keep its fundamental frequency above 50 Hz so everyday activity never drives it into resonance.

Supports were fixed at the top of each condyle, with a peak 1000 N bite applied vertically across the rear molars.

  • Fatigue life≥ 1,000,000 cycles
  • Natural frequency> 50 Hz
  • Peak load1000 N, rear molars
  • SupportsFixed at condyles
  • MaterialsTi-6Al-4V, SS316L

02 · Design

Material only where it works

A honeycomb lattice inside the plate and weight-reducing cut-outs remove mass from low-stress regions. Each geometry was analysed in both titanium and stainless steel.

03 · Results

Four variants, four passes

All four material and geometry combinations met every requirement. Next steps are fewer modelling assumptions and finer meshes, given more compute, for closer real-world accuracy.
