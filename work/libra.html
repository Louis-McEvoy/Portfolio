Libra

A smart scale that weighs, identifies and logs every food bank donation.

Timeline
Jan – Jun 2024
Recognition
Award-winning
Focus
Product design, vision
Fabrication
PCB, FDM, laser cutting

Problem

Food bank donations are logged by hand, often by older, untrained volunteers, leaving little usable data.

Approach

A scale with a camera on a jointed neck that weighs each item, identifies it via the ChatGPT API and uploads the record.

Result

An award-winning prototype that gives food banks evidence for funding requests.

01 · Context

Every donation, logged by hand

UK food banks are underfunded and understaffed, and donations are often recorded on paper by older, untrained volunteers.

Libra makes logging a single step: put the item down and it is weighed, identified and recorded. The data helps food banks track their activity and back funding requests with evidence.

02 · Form

Clean by design

A white acrylic body is easy to wipe down and shows dirt, so it stays hygienic. Electronics sit deep inside, away from spills.

An extendible base handles everything from tins to multipacks.

03 · Engineering

A camera that finds its angle

A jointed neck lets volunteers position the camera for a clean view. Each image goes to the ChatGPT API, which identifies the item before the record is uploaded with its weight.

A custom light-sensing PCB triggers capture. Internals, gears and the periscope were FDM printed; legs, base and frame were laser cut.

  • IdentificationCamera + ChatGPT API
  • TriggerLight-sensing PCB
  • PrintedPeriscope, gears, internals
  • Laser cutLegs, base, frame

04 · Use

Weigh, identify, upload

Placed on the platform, each item is weighed and photographed in one motion, with no forms to fill in.
