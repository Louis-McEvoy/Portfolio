Medi

An automated pill and water dispenser that takes medication rounds off carers’ hands.

Timeline
Jan – Jul 2025
Type
Team project
Focus
Research, mechatronics
Stack
ESP32, custom PCB, app

Watch the product video ↗

Problem

Caregivers spend too long managing medication, and human error puts elderly patients at risk.

Approach

An automated pill and water dispenser with touchscreen and audio reminders, plus an app for remote monitoring.

Result

A working prototype that holds three weeks of daily medication, shown in a product video.

01 · Research

Three countries, one routine

We interviewed caregivers in France, the UK and Hong Kong, and spoke to a geriatric doctor, a psychiatrist and a former social worker.

At Kippington nursing home in Sevenoaks we watched medication rounds, storage and delivery first-hand, then ran co-working sessions with caregivers and tore down competing products.

  • InterviewsCarers, clinicians
  • Site visitsCare homes
  • MarketsUK, France, Hong Kong
  • TeardownsCompetitor devices

02 · Product

Designed for a kitchen, not a clinic

Medi borrows from coffee machines so it blends into the home. A stepper-driven carousel holds three weeks of daily doses; servos release each dose and water, and light sensors confirm the pills have dropped.

A touchscreen and audio cues remind the patient when it is time.

  • ControllerESP32
  • Carousel1× slim NEMA 17 stepper
  • Release2× SG90 micro servo
  • Sensing4× LDR
  • Indicators4× LED
  • BoardCustom PCB
  • Capacity3 weeks of daily doses

03 · App

Carers can check in from anywhere

A paired app lets caregivers monitor doses remotely, so a missed dose is seen without a home visit.

04 · Build

Hundreds of parts, one board

A custom PCB brings the motors, sensors and display together cleanly. Using FDM printing and other rapid methods, we iterated through hundreds of individual component designs.
