Nocturne

A timid walking robot that senses light in 360° and runs from it.

Timeline
Oct – Dec 2024
Focus
Mechatronics, character
Electronics
Custom PCB, Arduino
Fabrication
FDM, laser cutting

Concept

An exploration of creative engineering, inspired by 1970s Soviet excavators and the desert robots of Star Wars.

Build

A recycled, hand-painted aluminium shell, semi-bipedal walking and four expressive eyes.

Behaviour

A 360° light-sensing PCB lets Nocturne detect light and flee from it.

01 · Mechanism

Walking, borrowed from animals

The legs follow animal physiology: printed “hamstrings” pull the foot through a natural step. A stepper motor drives a rack and pinion for the gait.

Four eyelids open and close from a single servo through a four-gear train.

  • Gait4× 28BYJ-48 stepper
  • Eyelids1× SG90 servo, 4 gears
  • Eyes4× LED
  • Sensing4× LDR, custom PCB
  • ControllerArduino Uno, C++

02 · Character

A machine you feel sorry for

Its eyes glow green when calm and red when angry. The timid behaviour and the distressed, hand-painted carapace draw out compassion rather than fear.

03 · Sensing

Light in, retreat out

A custom 360° light-sensing PCB on a rotating mast tells Nocturne where light is coming from, and it walks the other way. Behaviour is written in C++ on an Arduino Uno.
